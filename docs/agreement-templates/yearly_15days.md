### **1\. APPOINTMENT AND SCOPE OF SERVICES**

1.1 The Owner hereby appoints the Manager as an independent service provider to manage the Property. The Manager accepts this appointment and agrees to perform the services with due care and diligence. This Agreement creates a principal-to-principal relationship, and the Manager shall not be deemed an agent, lessee, licensee, or tenant of the Owner.

1.2 The Manager’s authority shall be strictly confined to the scope of services expressly defined under this Agreement and shall be contingent upon (i) the Owner’s prior written instructions or email consent, or (ii) a valid Power of Attorney (“PoA”) executed in favour of the Manager, for undertaking any act, omission, or representation falling outside such defined scope, including but not limited to the execution of documents, exercise of authority, or assumption of obligations on behalf of the Owner.

1.3 The scope of services provided by the Manager includes:

1) #### **Tenant Sourcing and Management:** The Manager shall be responsible for marketing the Property, screening potential tenants, and facilitating the execution of all tenancy agreements (whether a leave and license, rent, or tenancy agreement) strictly in the Owner’s name and with the Owner’s prior written approval. The Manager shall not sign or execute any such agreement in their own name. The Manager shall promptly report to the Owner any complaints, concerns, or grievances raised by tenants, and shall not provide any undertaking, concession, or commitment to the tenant without the Owner’s written consent.

2) #### **Property Maintenance and Repairs:** The Manager shall act solely as a liaison between the tenant and the Owner for all tenant-reported issues and shall coordinate and oversee only minor repairs and routine maintenance necessary to maintain the Property in a habitable condition. The tenant shall coordinate directly with the Manager for petty fixes and minor repair requirements, thereby reducing the Owner’s day-to-day involvement. All payments for such repairs or maintenance shall be made on actuals directly by either the Owner or the tenant. The Manager shall not advance or bear any repair or maintenance costs under any circumstances. The Manager shall not undertake any structural or major repairs, or incur any material expense, without the Owner’s prior written approval. The Manager shall notify the Owner within a reasonable period, and immediately in urgent or emergency situations, regarding any maintenance issues, tenant complaints, or observed damages. The Manager shall conduct a physical inspection of the Property upon the Owner’s request and provide a written inspection report to the Owner within seven (7) days of each inspection.

3) #### **Tenant Transition and Handover:** The Manager shall coordinate all tenant move-ins and move-outs, facilitate the complete handover of keys and assets (including the collection of keys and assets from vacating tenants), conduct comprehensive move-out checks of the Property, and document its condition through photographs or videos, where applicable, to ensure a seamless tenant transition. Upon any vacancy or receipt of a termination notice, the Manager shall promptly notify the Owner in writing if such notice is given by a tenant, and shall promptly notify the concerned tenant in writing if such notice is given by the Owner. The Manager shall not initiate or finalize the placement of a new tenant without the Owner’s prior written consent. The Owner shall have access to a consolidated dashboard providing real-time visibility into all property-related activities.

4) Tenant Replacement during the Term: Throughout the entire subsistence of this Agreement, in the event that a tenant vacates the Property or a tenancy is terminated, surrendered, or expires for any reason, the Manager shall, as an integral part of its services and without levying any additional, separate, or further charge over and above the Service Fee already payable under this Agreement, undertake to source, screen, and facilitate the placement of a suitable replacement tenant in accordance with Clause 1.3(a). The Owner shall not be liable to pay any additional finder’s fee, sourcing fee, re-tenanting fee, or similar charge to the Manager for securing such replacement tenant at any time during the Term. For the avoidance of doubt, this obligation requires the Manager to use reasonable and diligent efforts and does not guarantee any specific occupancy timeline, and any Owner-approved third-party marketing expenses shall continue to be governed by Clause 2.3.

### **2\. FINANCIAL TERMS AND REMUNERATION**

2.1 The Manager shall be entitled to a yearly service fee (“Service Fee”) equivalent to 15days of the one-month rent. The service fee will be applicable from \[EFFECTIVE DATE\]. In the event the house is vacant, the Owner will incur no charges payable to the Manager. 

2.1.1 The Service Fee shall be payable on an annual basis for each year of the Term. Accordingly, in respect of the Initial Term and each Renewal Term, the Owner shall pay to the Manager a Service Fee equivalent to fifteen (15) days of the prevailing monthly rent of the Property for that year.

2.1.2 The annual Service Fee for each renewed or newly agreed tenure may, at the Owner’s option, either be paid directly by the Owner to the Manager, or be adjusted against and paid out of the rental income received by the Owner in the first month of such renewed or newly agreed tenure, provided that neither Party has issued a notice to terminate or to discontinue this Agreement in accordance with Clause 3\. Where the Service Fee is so adjusted against the rental income, the Owner shall remit the corresponding amount to the Manager promptly upon receipt of such rent.

2.1.3 The Parties acknowledge and agree that the Service Fee is directly linked to the monthly rent payable for the Property and shall accordingly increase or decrease in proportion to any upward or downward revision in such rent. The Service Fee applicable for each year shall be computed on the basis of the monthly rent prevailing as on the date on which such Service Fee becomes due and payable.

2.2 The tenant(s) shall pay rent or monthly society maintenance charges directly to the Owner in accordance with the terms of the duly executed tenancy agreement. The Manager shall not, under any circumstances, collect, hold, or disburse rent or monthly society maintenance charges on behalf of the Owner. The Manager shall, however, notify the Owner in writing of the finalized rent amount and other key terms prior to the execution of each tenancy agreement and before tenant move-in. No rent or monthly society maintenance charges shall be due or payable to the Owner during any period in which the Property is vacant or unoccupied, and the Manager shall have no obligation to advance, guarantee, or pay rent, monthly society maintenance charges or any fixed amount to the Owner from its own funds during such periods.

2.3 All costs and expenses incurred by the Manager, under INR 1,000/month for fixing the property, marketing the Property and securing new tenants shall be initially borne by the Manager and reimbursed by the Owner. For expense of more than INR 1,000/month, a written approval from the Owner is needed.  
The Manager shall submit a written reimbursement request for such expenses on or before the First (1st) day of the following month, together with relevant supporting documents including invoices, bills, or authentic digital payment records (such as UPI confirmations or bank statements). The Owner shall reimburse the Manager for such marketing expenses along with the Service Fee of that same month, and in any event no later than the Fifth (5th) day of that month, together with the Service Fee for that month.

2.4 In the event of an emergency (including but not limited to burst pipes, electrical short circuits, gas leaks, or any situation that poses an immediate risk to the health or safety of the tenant(s) or to the structural integrity of the Property), the Manager shall promptly notify the Owner and the tenant and may coordinate with third-party vendors to facilitate immediate remedial measures. All payments for such emergency repairs shall be made directly by the Owner or the tenant, as applicable. The Manager shall not be liable for any loss, damage, or cost arising from such emergencies, except where directly caused by the Manager’s gross negligence or willful misconduct. Manager cannot approve any repair above INR 5,000 without Owner’s written approval (except life-threatening emergencies).
