### **2. FINANCIAL TERMS AND REMUNERATION**

2.1 The Manager shall be entitled to a service fee (“Service Fee”) equivalent to **three (3) days’ rent** for every one (1) month’s rent, computed as the total monthly consideration (rent) payable by the tenant divided by thirty (30) and multiplied by three (3) (i.e., ten percent (10%) of such monthly consideration), as specified in the then-effective leave and license agreement duly executed with the tenant(s). The Service Fee shall be applicable for the Initial Term and shall continue to apply, automatically, for each Renewal Term during the subsistence of this Agreement. In the event of any modification, amendment, renewal, or extension of the leave and license agreement, or any other revision resulting in an increase or decrease in the monthly consideration, the Service Fee shall correspondingly increase or decrease and shall be computed on the basis of the revised monthly consideration prevailing for the relevant month.

2.2 All rent payable by the tenant(s) in respect of the Property shall be paid through the Manager’s digital application or platform (the “Platform”). The Manager shall share with the Owner, on or before the Fifth (5th) day of each month, a statement (through the Platform or by email) setting out the rent received, the Service Fee retained and the balance remitted to the Owner for the preceding month. The Service Fee shall be inclusive of all applicable taxes, which shall be absorbed by the Manager in accordance with prevailing laws.

2.2A The monthly Service Fee shall be discharged in the following manner:

> (a) Automatic deduction through the Platform: the tenant shall pay the monthly rent through the Platform, and the Manager shall be entitled to automatically deduct and retain its Service Fee from the amount so received and remit the balance to the Owner’s bank account specified in Annexure 1 within three (3) business days of receipt. The deduction of the Service Fee in this manner shall constitute valid and full discharge of the Owner’s payment obligation for that month; and
>
> (b) Rent received outside the Platform: where, for any month, any rent is paid by the tenant directly to the Owner or otherwise outside the Platform, the Owner shall promptly inform the Manager in writing and shall pay the Service Fee for that month to the Manager within five (5) days of receipt of such rent, against the invoice raised by the Manager.

2.3 The tenant(s) shall pay rent through the Platform in accordance with Clause 2.2A, and monthly society maintenance charges directly to the Owner, in accordance with the terms of the duly executed tenancy agreement. The Manager shall, however, notify the Owner in writing of the finalized rent amount and other key terms prior to the execution of each tenancy agreement and before tenant move-in. No rent or monthly society maintenance charges shall be due or payable to the Owner during any period in which the Property is vacant or unoccupied, and the Manager shall have no obligation to advance, guarantee, or pay rent, monthly society maintenance charges or any fixed amount to the Owner from its own funds during such periods.

2.4 All costs and expenses incurred by the Manager for marketing the Property and securing new tenants shall be borne solely by the Manager, and the Owner shall not be liable to reimburse or pay any such marketing expenses.

2.5 In the event of an emergency (including but not limited to burst pipes, electrical short circuits, gas leaks, or any situation that poses an immediate risk to the health or safety of the tenant(s) or to the structural integrity of the Property), the Manager shall promptly notify the Owner and the tenant and may coordinate with third-party vendors to facilitate immediate remedial measures. All payments for such emergency repairs shall be made directly by the Owner or the tenant, as applicable. The Manager shall not be liable for any loss, damage, or cost arising from such emergencies, except where directly caused by the Manager’s gross negligence or willful misconduct. Manager cannot approve any repair above INR 10,000 without Owner’s written approval (except life-threatening emergencies).
